# Walder 0.2.2

A bug-fix and legibility release. The headline is that Walder stopped moving: a single
wrong answer in the animation clock froze him on the first frame of his own blink, and
froze him again after every click. One line, three of the reports below.

## Fixed
- **He no longer freezes.** The frame clock reported "nothing will ever change again" whenever an animation was swapped mid-paint, so no timer was armed. The shipped blink runs half-closed → closed → half-closed, which is why he was found parked mid-blink; the pet wiggle a click plays did the same thing at the other end, leaving him still for the rest of the session. He now blinks all day and returns to the idle loop after every click.
- **The hover card stays put.** It was measured against the sprite frame currently on screen, so a blink, a bark or a pet wiggle moved it and the card followed him frame by frame. It is now anchored to his resting pose and only moves when the window is dragged, the size changes, or he turns round.
- **The currency symbol is on both halves** of a money row: `€9.62 / €50.00`, not `9.62 / €50.00`. A usage row is two facts side by side, and the number the eye lands on first is the spend.

## Changed
- **Bubbles stay until you click him.** Every kind — a bark, a `woof`, the `?`, the update notice. Walder polls every three minutes, and a bubble that showed itself for twelve seconds of that was a warning you received only if you happened to be looking at the corner of the screen. The `…zzz` a pet earns from a sleeping dog still fades on its own, because a click is what makes it.
- **A higher threshold replaces its own bark in place.** An 80% bubble waiting to be clicked becomes an 85% one rather than letting 85 queue invisibly behind it. A *different* window still waits its turn.
- **The speech bubble is sized for reading, not for the dog** — 12 / 14 / 16 px at Small / Medium / Large, where it used to be a fixed multiple of the sprite and shrank to 8 px alongside him. The window's reserve above him is derived from the same number, so the two cannot drift.
- **The hover card is wider** (380 px at Large), so the `Codex credit limit` row's title is no longer eaten by its own value.
- **Extra usage has a reset line again**, reading `resets in 19d 3h (est.)`. claude.ai's response states a monthly cap and a monthly spend and no date at all, so this one horizon is Walder's arithmetic — the first of the next calendar month — and the `(est.)` marks it as the only figure on the card that is not the provider's.

## New
- **Primary service** in the tray menu: Claude or ChatGPT. That service's rows lead the hover card, and when several windows cross a threshold in the same poll its bark is the one you see first. It deliberately does not change the dog's face, which always tracks the Claude 5-hour window.
