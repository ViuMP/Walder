# Walder 0.2.8

The release where Walder gets a housemate. Yuna is a cat — five coats of her own, each one
illustrated frame by frame and approved on 2026-09-21 — and she is picked the same way a coat is.
Nothing about the dog changed: his sheet, his timings and his behaviour are byte-for-byte what
0.2.7 shipped.

## New

- **Yuna, in five coats: grey tabby, orange tabby, black, tuxedo and calico.** They sit at the
  bottom of **Colour**, under the six Walder coats, and switch the same way — the choice is
  remembered across restarts, and swapping mid-animation lands on a valid frame rather than
  jumping.
- **She has her own animations, not recoloured dog ones.** Her perk is six frames of batting a red
  yarn ball and then holds the last two; her tail wag is three. Everything the dog does she does in
  her own drawings — the moods, the sleep curl, the lie-down postures, the bark, the walk, the hop,
  the petting.
- Her `tilt` carries no baked `?` and her `sleep` no baked `z z`, so the app draws the same shared
  glyphs over her that it draws over Walder.

## Fixed

- **A slow Walder no longer holds up Claude Code.** The hooks were installed as synchronous, so
  every prompt waited up to a second on Walder's local listener when it was busy. They are now
  installed with `"async": true`. An existing install is not upgraded on its own: run **Install
  Claude Code hooks…** once more and it rewrites Walder's entries in place, adds no duplicates and
  touches nothing else in `~/.claude/settings.json`.
- **The handbook's Silver dapple swatch was a broken image.** The coat row was drawn from one list
  and the pictures from another, and the second was never updated when the dapple landed in 0.2.6.
  Both now come from the sheet itself, so a coat the artwork adds can no longer arrive without its
  picture — which is also how the five Yuna swatches got there.

## Moved

- **Walder's source is public, and everything now lives at
  [github.com/ViuMP/Walder](https://github.com/ViuMP/Walder)** — releases (every earlier one was
  copied over), bug reports and security reports. The update check and **Report a bug…** point
  there from this version on. The old `walder-releases` repository is archived; 0.2.8 is its last
  release, so that installs still checking it are told where to look.

## Notes

- **Apple Silicon only**, unsigned: **System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Open Anyway**, then
  **Done**.
- Yuna is a second *character*, carried as five more frame sets plus a small per-set animation
  override; a sheet without that override — any older one — behaves exactly as it did before.
- No manual pixels ship in this release. One owner-requested correction to the grey tabby's eyes
  was made under the new exception in `CONTRIBUTING.md` and rejected; the generated art stands.
  `docs/handoffs/CLAUDE_YUNA.md` logs it.
