# Walder 0.2.9

The release after two full QA passes over 0.2.8, one on a Mac and one on a Windows PC, every row of
`docs/QA-CHECKLIST.md` driven on screen. Nothing new to learn: everything here is something 0.2.8
did wrong, found by using it.

## Fixed — both platforms

- **He comes back where you left him.** Quitting while he was asleep or lying down relaunched him
  lower down the screen; a pet on a widened bubble could shift the saved spot sideways.
- **Bubbles stay on screen.** At the default corner the hello and the barks ran off the right edge;
  a bubble now widens into the room that is on screen and is laid out again when the window moves.
- **A size change keeps his whole body on screen**, at the corner too, and no longer shifts him
  while a bubble is up. **Reset position** lands at 16 px from the edges even with a bubble up.
- **The hover card stays put** through a bark and the pet that clears it, comes down when the cursor
  leaves him (also when the system never reports the leave, e.g. after a drag that ends on him), and
  the card's primary service is the first section, as the setting always said.
- **A blink no longer flickers the hover**, and a lost mouse-up no longer leaves a drag hanging.
- **The first-run hello cannot be pushed away by a bark.** A usage bark on the first poll used to
  replace "Click the bone…" for good; it now waits behind it, and the hooks offer opens with its own
  sentence, not one click early.
- **A held pose survives a posture change.** When the weekly pool laid him down or stood him up
  while a `?`, a `… done` or a bark was showing, the tilt, the ears-up or the bark itself was dropped
  while the bubble stayed.
- **After a sleep the numbers stay.** A wake before Wi-Fi was back emptied the card and confused his
  face, then backed off for minutes; now the last good numbers stay with their age, the poll waits
  for the network, and a failed read is retried every 25 s for two minutes.
- **One bad setting costs only that setting** (it used to reset the whole settings file); one log
  line per bad key.
- **Logging out reads as logged out at once**, the tray's login line included, and the numbers that
  login fed leave the card.
- **Hooks:** real `Stop` and `PostToolUse` bodies over 8 KiB are accepted; the tray reflects an
  install or removal at once; the first-launch offer no longer blocks the app or appears before he
  is drawn; the Codex offer says to trust the hooks with `/hooks`.
- One **Tokens today** row after a relaunch, not two; the restored card keeps its order; the
  credit row on the Large card wraps instead of cutting its label.

## Fixed — macOS

- **Launch at login no longer adds a second entry** with every new build (an unsigned build is a
  new app to macOS each time). If System Settings ▸ General ▸ Login Items lists Walder twice from an
  older version, remove the extras there.
- A pet over a fullscreen film no longer wakes him for good.

## Fixed — Windows

- **He sleeps over fullscreen video.** The fullscreen helper never compiled on Windows, so 0.2.8
  never noticed a film.
- **Crisp and grounded at 125 %/150 % scaling**: he was drawn smaller than his box, floated above
  the taskbar and took clicks a few pixels off.
- **The tray menu is fresh on every open**, right-click included (it showed the previous poll's
  labels).
- The hooks command exits cleanly when Walder is not running (Claude Code showed every hook as
  failed), and writing `settings.json` is retried while Claude Code has it open.
- A Windows path on the card's SESSIONS line is shortened at `\` like a Mac path at `/`.

## Notes

- **macOS: Apple Silicon only**, unsigned: **System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Open Anyway**,
  then **Done**.
- **Windows: x64**, unsigned: SmartScreen warns that it does not recognise the app; click **More
  info**, then **Run anyway**.
- Known: on some accounts chatgpt.com answers a fresh login with a banner instead of usage; ChatGPT
  is then read through the Codex CLI login, as before.
- The QA reports are in `docs/handoffs/` (`QA_0.2.8_REPORT.md`, `QA_0.2.8_WINDOWS_REPORT.md`,
  `QA_0.2.8_MAC_RECHECK.md`).
