## v0.4.0: seven languages and an intel mac download

2026-11-21

Walder now speaks seven languages, and there is a download for Intel Macs. Also every fix from the Mac and Windows QA rounds of 2026-11-18 to 2026-11-20.

Unsigned installers for macOS on Apple Silicon, macOS on Intel and Windows x64; see the [first-launch steps](https://github.com/ViuMP/Walder).

### Added
- **Seven languages**: English, Danish, Simplified Chinese, Hindi, Spanish, French and German. App ▸ **Language** follows your computer by default, or picks one of the seven; a change applies after a restart. The six translations were made by machine and have not yet been read by a native speaker, so please send corrections with the [translation form](https://github.com/ViuMP/Walder/issues/new?template=translation.yml). Logs, the bug-report block and the settings file stay in English. (#31, #36)
- **Intel Mac download**: `Walder-mac-x64.dmg`, next to the Apple Silicon one. **Not yet tested on Intel hardware**: if you have an Intel Mac, please report whether it starts. (#38)
- **Out-of-date hooks bubble**: when Walder's installed Claude Code or Codex hooks were written by an older Walder, the dog says "Update Claude Code hooks" or "Update Codex hooks" once after the update that made them stale, not on every launch, and never ahead of the Install or Reinstall notices. The bone menu reads **Update … hooks…**, its status line says "out of date", and the question it opens asks to Update. (#36, #38, #40)
- **Developer ▸ Simulate account state** (development builds and `WALDER_DEV=1` only): Codex credits, extra usage and spend limits, in memory only. (#28)
- **Handbook online**: the handbook is now at https://viump.github.io/Walder/ and is kept up to date with every change to it. (#36)

### Changed
- **Hidden services make no requests**: a service switched off in Show in overview is no longer checked, including its login check and Claude's CLI renewal; switching it back on checks it at once. A hidden Claude no longer leaves the dog's face on an old number once its 5-hour window resets. (#28)
- **Remove hooks**: Cancel is the default button. (#28)
- **Petting while Claude Code waits**: clears the `?` and plays the pet, but no longer brings the terminal to the front, as with Codex. (#30)
- **Resting at the top edge**: the screen rule measures the drawn dog, not his whole frame, so the dachshund rests a little nearer the top of the screen. Yuna rests where she did, so her bubbles keep their lines. At Small on a 175 % or 250 % display, a dachshund at the top edge has room for one bubble line instead of two. (#36)
- **SESSIONS**: at most five rows, then "+N more". Two Codex sessions get two rows. (#36)
- **Tokens today**: the two rows read "Claude tokens today" and "Codex tokens today". (#36)
- **When a server asks Walder to wait**, every service now follows the same rule. (#36)
- **Percentages and French punctuation** follow each language's conventions (no-break spaces where they belong). (#36)

### Fixed
- **Petting no longer takes keyboard focus** (macOS): after a pet, the app you were typing in gets the keyboard back. (#38)
- **A pet that wobbles** no longer moves him or saves a new spot, and a pet no longer changes which side he keeps when his size changes. (#38)
- **Hover card over menus**: the card stays off the bone menu, also when it is opened from the tray icon, and comes back when the menu closes with the pointer still on him. (#36, #38)
- **Size changes at an edge**: a size change and back at the top edge, or with him flush against the left or right side, now ends where he started. Before, Small → Medium → Large → Small at the top ended 144 pt lower, and Large → Small → Large at a side ended 16 pt in. (#36, #40)
- **Installing hooks from the menu** takes the "Install … hooks" bubble down at once, instead of leaving it up until you pet him. When a Codex hooks file cannot be read, the result no longer tells you to trust hooks that were not installed. (#40)
- **After the dog window crashes and reloads**, he comes back in the pose his bubble held: ears up under a "done", head cocked under a `?`. (#40)
- **Bubbles no longer make him jump**: a bark no longer resizes the dog's window, so the frame where he jumped a few pixels aside, or the bubble showed cut off or wrapped onto two lines as it came up or went, is gone. The window now keeps room beside him for a bubble; it is see-through and clicks pass through it as before. On a Mac, moving the mouse quickly right beside him costs a little more CPU than before (about 3–4 % of one core while it moves). (#40)
- **ChatGPT login**: an anonymous chatgpt.com visitor no longer counts as logged in, so the login window stays open until you have signed in. (#34)
- **Dragging at 125 %** (Windows) no longer grows the window a little with every move. (#32)
- **Gemini on Windows**: found through Antigravity, as on the Mac. (#32)
- **The Windows hook question** stays in front of other windows, and clicking the menu item again brings it forward. (#36)
- **Coat names** are translated in every language, and they, the Overview's Refresh row and the Hindi up-to-date notice now fit. (#38)
- **⌘W** closes the Walder window. (#38)
- **Report a bug…** fills in your processor on the form. (#38)
- **Notify when hidden**: a notification that went missing right after ticking it now shows. (#38)
- **Keyboard focus in the Walder window** is kept after a setting is saved, and both card previews fit a narrow window without scrolling sideways. (#32)
- **Installer contents**: tinyglobby, fdir and picomatch, left out in 0.3.0, are back in the app archive as unused packages, now that picomatch 4.0.7 fixed their advisory; no known advisories remain. (#36)
